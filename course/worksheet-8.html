    Problem 1.

    Remember the Intermediate Value Theorem (IVT)? It says: If \(f\) is a continuous function on the closed interval \([a,b]\text{,}\) then \(f\) attains every value between \(f(a)\) and \(f(b)\) somewhere on the interval \([a,b]\text{.}\)
    🔗
    This theorem and Newton’s method can work together to tell us about roots of really unruly functions like
    \begin{equation*} f(x) = x^{5} - \frac{5}{2}x^2 + x - \frac{3}{2}. \end{equation*}
    🔗
    1. How do we know we aren’t wasting our time looking for a root? The IVT can tell us that one exists. Fill in the blanks:
      🔗
      Since \(f(x)\) is on \([0,2]\) and
      \begin{equation*} f(0) = \underline{\hspace{0.5in}} \lt \underline{\hspace{0.5in}} \lt f(2) = \underline{\hspace{0.5in}}, \end{equation*}
      the IVT guarantees that there is at least one solution to the equation \(f(x) =\) . That is, \(f(x)\) has a on \([0,2]\text{.}\)
      🔗
      🔗
    2. Now that we know there is a root, we can use Newton’s method to estimate it. Use \(x_0 = 1\) to find an estimate \(x_1\) of the root.
      🔗
      🔗
    3. Examine the graph of \(f(x)\text{.}\) Is \(x_1\) closer to the root in \([0,2]\text{?}\) Can you find a different choice of \(x_0\) that might work better to approximate the root of \(f\text{?}\) Find \(x_1\) using the graph of \(f(x)\) and Newton’s method with your chosen \(x_0\text{.}\)
      🔗
      🔗

    Problem 3.

    A culture of yeast cells is introduced to a growth medium. For the first two hours (\(0 \leq t \leq 2\)), the population of the culture at time \(t\) (in hours) is modeled by
    \begin{equation*} P(t) = 10{,}000 + 500t + \frac{1000}{\pi}\cos(\pi t). \end{equation*}
    🔗
    1. Find the rate of change of the population, \(P'(t)\text{,}\) at any time \(t\text{.}\)
      🔗
      🔗
    2. At what exact time \(t\) during the first two hours (\(0 \leq t \leq 2\)) does the population stop increasing for the first time?
